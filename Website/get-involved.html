Get Involved

Start A Local Chapter

Step 1

Submit an Application

Submit an application detailing the ways your chapter will benefit the FLOTE organization and attract new, passionate members. Include any past involvement with FLOTE or experience with other nonprofit organizations.

Step 2

Choose Leadership Positions

Choose positions for board members of your chapter and detail their respective experience in their field.
Examples of leadership positions for FLOTE Chapters include:

  • Executive Director
  • Volunteer/Events Manager
  • Fundraising Manager
  • Social Media/Outreach Manager
  • Membership Manager
Step 3

Establish Your Local Chapter

Set up your chapter website and/or social media channels to increase your chapter’s visibility and attract new members to your chapter.
