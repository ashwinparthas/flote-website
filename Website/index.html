Who Are We?

Our mission is to empower young people to create a positive change in their communities while connecting to their cultural heritage.

Our goal is to create more well-informed members of society to contribute to a better understanding of global affairs and cultures.

Kamala Harris Announced as Joe Biden's Running Mate

August 11, 2020

Democratic Presidential Nominee Joe Biden has announced that California Senator Kamala Harris will be his Vice President. Harris will be the first woman of African or Tamil descent to be selected as the vice-presidential nominee of a major American party.

Canadian PM Justin Truedeau Honors Black July Victims

July 23, 2020

Prime Minister Trudeau honored victims of Black July, a 1983 ethnic pogrom that contributed to the deaths of thousands of Tamils, displaced over 100,000 more, and led to the destruction of millions of dollars worth of Tamil businesses and homes.

Read More

US Secretary of State Mike Pompeo Sanctions SL Lieutenant General Shavendra Silva from Entry into the United States

February 14, 2020

FLOTE commends United States Secretary of State Mike Pompeo for designating Sri Lankan Lieutenant General Shavendra Silva ineligible for entry into the United States. During the end of the conflict in Sri Lanka, there have been credible allegations of his involvement in extrajudicial killings and severe violations of human rights.
