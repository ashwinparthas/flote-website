Never Let a Day Pass Without a Good Deed - 38th Kural

Community service is the cornerstone of FLOTE's mission to empower young people to create a positive change in their communities. From volunteering with a local nonprofit to starting your own charitable enterprise, FLOTE is committed to backing our members in their altruistic pursuits.

By uniting youth across the world with a mission of spreading compassion, FLOTE aspires to instill the virtues of altruism and philanthropy in a new generation of leaders.

Alongside working with community organizations of diverse backgrounds, FLOTE sponsors our own community service enterprises. These enterprises include hosting food drives, complimentary online tutoring, and pro bono college admissions consulting.

Step 2

FLOTE Pro Bono College Consulting

Knowledge is power. Information is liberating. Education is the premise of progress, in every society, in every family.

- Kofi Annan, former Secretary-General of the United Nations

The college application process can be confusing and is often a daunting task for many. Students who will be the first in their family to apply to college, come from immigrant communities, or low-income families tend to face obstacles that further exacerbate existing challenges. Recognizing this and the importance of education, FLOTE is committed to providing free of charge college admissions consulting to ensure that all students maximize their potential, regardless of who they are or where they come from.

FLOTE has an incredible team of students and graduates who are experienced in the college admissions process. Staying true to our mission to help all, our volunteers offer both in-person and virtual sessions to ensure our services are accessible by as many students as possible.

Volunteers will work with our members on a scheduled basis to explain the admissions process and answer related questions. Moreover, our team will provide assistance in identifying scholarship opportunities, preparing for standardized testing, and writing application essays. Our Pro Bono College Consulting program is open to all high school students.
