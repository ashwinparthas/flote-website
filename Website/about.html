About FLOTE

Who Are We?

Our mission is to empower young people to create a positive change in their communities while connecting to their cultural heritage.

FLOTE actively works to organize and present Tamil history and current events in order to reinforce cultural ties among youth in the diaspora and to educate all about Tamil culture. FLOTE chapters actively initiate or assist in volunteer service projects to instill in youth the importance of community service and philanthropy.

Our goal is to create more well-informed members of society to contribute to a better understanding of global affairs and cultures.

FLOTE educates and empowers its local chapters to advocate on behalf of issues facing the Tamil community abroad. Our primary goal is to promote justice through the international recognition of the genocide against Tamils in Sri Lanka.
